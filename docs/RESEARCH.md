# Interface research notes

The revamp was designed against repeated “AI slop” patterns documented across interface-design criticism and the user-provided launch research.

## Rejected defaults

- Purple-to-blue decorative gradients
- Inter as an unconsidered universal typeface
- Centered headline, subtitle, two buttons, and three equal feature cards
- Glass surfaces, excessive radii, floating dashboard mockups, and fake social proof
- Motion applied uniformly instead of communicating state
- Provider routing hidden behind a generic “Auto” label

## Chosen direction

- Editorial display typography paired with a disciplined interface grotesk
- Warm black, paper, and one operational chartreuse signal
- Asymmetric narrative layouts and hairline structural grids
- Procedural 3D tied specifically to planner-to-executor routing
- An actual interactive task workspace as the primary demonstration
- Visible provider access method, privacy state, route reason, budget ceiling, and acceptance checks

## Sources reviewed

- Awwwards technology and 3D interface examples
- Current writing on why AI-generated sites converge on component-library defaults
- RouteLLM, OpenRouter, Not Diamond, LiteLLM, and Requesty product patterns
- User-supplied discussions covering launch stability, security, formal testing, review bottlenecks, and beta feedback

Research influenced the visual system and the product controls; no external site layout or distinctive brand asset was copied.
